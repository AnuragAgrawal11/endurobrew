01 · The problem

Three gaps in urban India

Professionals and players want fast, clean, high-protein fuel. Today they get queues, sugary sodas or broken machines.

Cafe bottleneck

Queueing, ordering and waiting takes 15–20 minutes for a basic coffee or post-workout drink.

Gym / turf void

After pickleball, padel or turf cricket it's warm sugary soft drinks or a messy protein shaker.

Vending failure

Old machines are clunky, eat money, jam and need cash or tokens.

The 1-click fix

Scan QR → pick on a web page → 1-tap UPI → the tap pours or the glass door unlocks. Zero staff on site.

What Yogurtland Dubai & UK froyo sell

Self-serve, pay-by-weight froyo is booming in both markets. Here's what they offer and what EnduroBrew can borrow.

Yogurtland · Dubai

UAE · since 2014
  • Opened at The Dubai Mall in Jan 2014; also Springs Souk and BoxPark, plus Sharjah.
  • Flavours seen: mango, pistachio, macadamia, white chocolate, blue raspberry; vegan chocolate and sorbets (often sold out).
  • Toppings: fresh fruit, tapioca pearls, crushed biscuits; sauces added at the till.
  • Model: fully self-serve, cup weighed at the counter. From AED 2.99 per ounce; ~AED 32.50 for a medium cup with three toppings.
  • Brand-wide: up to 16 rotating flavours, ~30 toppings, non-fat, low-fat, non-dairy and no-sugar-added options; oat-milk flavours; açaí & fruit bowls; a pineapple + spinach + kale "greens" smoothie bowl.

UK froyo scene

London · 2026 boom

Yogurtland doesn't list the UK among its markets, so these are the UK leaders it would compete with.

07 · Personalised fuel

Your workout picks your drink

Arjun's idea + Anurag's face login: read wearable data, work out what each person needs today, and have the machine make it — protein, electrolytes, carbs, caffeine or none.

1 · Sync

Free EnduroBrew app

Reads Apple Health (iOS) or Health Connect (Android), plus WHOOP and Garmin accounts. No watch? A 3-tap quiz: sport, minutes, how hard.
2 · Compute

Fuel Engine

Turns today's workout, sleep, body weight, goal, diet and venue weather into a recipe — always inside safe ranges set with a sports dietitian.
3 · Identify

QR first, face later

Show a 30-second QR from the app at the machine. Opt-in face "Express" for regulars at gyms, with a phone tap to confirm.
4 · Dispense

Machine makes it

Base (shake, smoothie bowl, froyo, cold brew, electrolyte) + dosing pumps add exact protein, electrolytes, carbs or a caffeine shot.

What the machine needs

Hardware added for personalisation

  • Camera for QR (and later face, with an edge-AI chip)
  • Touch screen showing name, recipe, Accept / Swap
  • 4–6 peristaltic dosing pumps: protein concentrate, electrolyte, carb syrup, espresso shot, flavour
  • Flow meters + load cell to verify every dose
  • NFC reader (phase 2) and 4G modem for uptime
What the software adds

On top of your existing stack

  • Mobile app (iOS + Android) with HealthKit & Health Connect SDKs
  • Wearable connectors: WHOOP & Garmin cloud APIs
  • Fuel Engine: rules + caps first, ML on preferences later
  • Identity service: signed rotating QR, UPI-ID link, face templates
  • Consent manager: DPDP-style notices, per-purpose opt-in, delete-my-data

Why it fixes the volume risk

Subscriptions

A monthly "Fuel Pass" with daily drink credits turns one-off buyers into daily regulars — the 30 cups/day pilot target gets much easier. Lower-ticket protein & electrolyte shots also open the mass protein-powder market, as Arjun suggested.
Partner model

Brand cartridges

Protein and electrolyte brands supply sealed cartridges and pay for placement or data-free co-marketing. Customers pick "brand X protein" on screen; you earn a margin plus a listing fee.
Benchmark

LINC (US)

A home "connected supplement system": survey baseline, Apple Health / WHOOP / Oura sync, a daily drink adjusted within safety ranges. Waitlist only, adults only. EnduroBrew = the same idea as a public machine network for India, food-first (shakes, bowls, froyo) rather than supplements.

India reality check

  • Most Indian smartwatches are budget brands: Noise (26.6%), boAt (14.1%) and Fire-Boltt (9.7%) led 2025 shipments. Test whether their apps write to Health Connect — if not, the quiz path must be great.
  • Apple Health has no server API: data comes through our iOS app, on-device.
  • Garmin reopened its developer program on 21 Oct 2026 with Personal, Starter and Enterprise tiers — apply early.
  • WHOOP has a developer platform; members must opt in to share.

Privacy by design (DPDP Rules 2025)

  • India's DPDP Rules were notified in Nov 2025 with an 18-month phased rollout: short consent notices, clear purposes, Indian-language options.
  • Face data and health data are personal data: separate, explicit opt-in for each; easy delete.
  • Keep raw wearable data on the phone; send only today's "fuel needs" to the cloud.
  • Face: store an encrypted template, never photos; match on the machine; always a non-face option.
  • Lab reports: later, reviewed by a dietitian partner; wellness advice, not medical treatment.
